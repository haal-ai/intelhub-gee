# intelhub-gee

**GEE (GenAI Engineering Excellence) Technology Radar** — intelligence gathering for the GEE team.

## Purpose

This hub is the GEE team's technology radar for tracking technologies, tools, processes, and trends relevant to GenAI engineering. It feeds into the central hub [intelhub-genai-central](https://github.com/haal-ai/intelhub-genai-central).

## What belongs here

- Technologies, tools, and processes the GEE team is watching or evaluating
- GenAI-related trends, frameworks, protocols, and patterns relevant to engineering excellence
- Items at any ring (adopt, trial, assess, hold) that the GEE team has an opinion on

## What does NOT belong here

- Internal innovation projects or PoCs (those go in `inovhub`)
- Items that are purely aggregated from other teams (those go in the central hub)

## Hub role

`team` — this hub owns its own intells directly. High-impact items may be promoted to the central hub.

Central hub: [intelhub-genai-central](https://github.com/haal-ai/intelhub-genai-central)
